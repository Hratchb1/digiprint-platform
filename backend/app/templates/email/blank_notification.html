Why would my film turn out blank?

Extreme underexposure

Very fast shutter, very small aperture, or ISO set far too high can leave frames effectively blank.

Tip: match ISO to the film box speed and use a light meter or auto mode in low light. If this was a disposable camera, the photos most likely were used in low light conditions without flash.

Unshot roll was developed

A fresh roll got processed by mistake. Result is completely clear film with edge markings.

Tip: mark exposed rolls and keep fresh rolls in their canisters.

Camera opened mid-roll

Opening the back floods the film with light. You might see uniform fog rather than images.

Tip: only open the back after rewinding fully.

Shutter didn't fire or is stuck

Mechanical fault or low battery on electronic shutters.

Tip: replace the battery and fire a few test shots before your next roll.

Film didn't load or advance

The leader didn't catch on the take-up spool. The counter moved, but the film didn't.
